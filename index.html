What we do

The SRVU pillars

Advocacy, service and development. It sounds like policy language. In practice it means someone is in the room when decisions about your degree get made.

Advocacy

USR · LSVb · Campaigns

Advocacy happens through our council members in the University Student Council (USR), the highest student participation body at the VU. Nationally we work through the Dutch National Student Union (LSVb).

And when the formal route stalls, we campaign: petitions, actions and press, together with students and staff.

Service

Legal aid · Student desk · Room office

Free legal advice on education, tenancy and employment law. A student desk for every question that doesn't fit anywhere else. An exchange market and a menstrual cabinet in the Studentendok.

Go to legal advice →

Development

Facilities · Board year · Committees

We push the university towards optimal facilities, and we give students room to grow: a board year, a committee seat, or a first campaign of your own.

About us

Student union of the VU since 1947

The SRVU has represented students at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam since 1947. As the representative of VU students we're there for everyone, member or not, to stand up for their interests.

Alongside that we provide services, push the university forward and give students room to develop themselves. The SRVU stands for more than just studying.

How we're organised

University Student Council

Our council members sit in the USR, the highest participation body at the VU, elected by students every spring.

LSVb

Through the Dutch National Student Union we take VU issues to The Hague and act with unions across the country.
